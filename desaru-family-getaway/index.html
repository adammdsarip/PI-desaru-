The Experience

More Than a Getaway. Time Together.

Step away from the everyday and enjoy a family getaway filled with nature, good food and shared experiences. Discover the charm of Desaru, explore the farm and end the evening with the magic of a firefly cruise.

  • Nature

    A farm visit by day and a river cruise through firefly-lit mangroves by night.

  • Good Food

    An exclusive kambing golek lunch at Aliyah Rizq Farm, plus dinner at Fruitfly Kelong.

  • Quality Time

    Transport, stay and guiding are arranged, so the weekend is about being together.

FAQ

Good to Know

Can’t find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

What are the trip dates and duration?

The getaway runs for 2 days and 1 night, from Saturday 9 to Sunday 10 January 2027.

What is included in the package?

Return coach transport between Singapore and Desaru, a tour guide, one night at Tunamaya Desaru (standard twin-share), the Aliyah Rizq Farm tour with entrance, buggy ride and baby sheep & goat photo op, an exclusive farm lunch, the Sungai Lebam River firefly cruise, one dinner at Fruitfly Kelong, a shopping stop in Johor Bahru and group travel insurance. See the full list.

Are horse riding and ATV rides included?

No. Horse riding and ATV rides are optional activities at your own expense. Please ask the organiser for current prices.

What are the accommodation arrangements?

One night at Tunamaya Desaru in a standard twin-share room. Accommodation is subject to availability or change. For room arrangements for families with children, please check with the organiser.

Is the itinerary fixed?

Itineraries are subject to change, and notice will be given before any change. Exact timings will be shared by the organiser.

What is excluded from the package?

Tipping for the tour guide or driver, personal expenses, and the optional horse riding and ATV rides.

How do I enquire or confirm a booking?

Send us a message on WhatsApp and the Pure Intent team will share package details and availability. Sending an enquiry does not reserve a place — your booking is confirmed only when the organiser confirms it with you.

Are child rates or special arrangements available?

Child rates and special arrangements have not been published. Please contact the organiser to ask about your family’s needs.
